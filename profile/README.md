## About Tinvex

Tinvex is a fan app and the operating system for communities. Fans pick their people (a club, an artist, a team), join with a digital membership card, earn XP with every move and redeem rewards that money can't buy, from Bronze to Diamond.

## For clubs, artists and creators

Got an audience? Tinvex turns followers into superfans: digital membership card and season ticket on the phone, XP and tiers that reward loyal fans, your own community with no algorithms in the way, and exclusive rewards and experiences. Learn more at https://tinvex.io/en/comunidad/
