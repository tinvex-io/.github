# Code of Conduct

Tinvex is built around communities, and our projects should be welcoming to everyone who takes part in them.

## Our standards

Be respectful, kind and constructive. Assume good intentions, give and accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing other people's private information and any other behavior that makes people feel unsafe or unwelcome are not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Tinvex spaces: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks it.

To report a problem, email contacto@tinvex.io. All reports are handled with discretion.
